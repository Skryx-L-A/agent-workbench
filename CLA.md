## How to accept

Comment on your pull request with exactly this sentence:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement and I agree to it.

Your acceptance applies to this pull request. Each Contribution You submit to this repository
after Your acceptance is on record is a separate acceptance of this agreement for that
Contribution. You are not obliged to submit anything. You can stop this at any time by telling
the Maintainer in text form (for example by e-mail). After your first accepted pull request, the Maintainer adds your GitHub account
to [CLA-SIGNATURES.md](CLA-SIGNATURES.md), and the check passes automatically for your later
pull requests to this repository.
